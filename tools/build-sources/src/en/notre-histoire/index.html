The House

Two houses, one soul

Two houses, one history

Tucked into the Fès medina, steps from Bab Boujloud and the Batha district, two traditional houses carried their story through the 20th century before becoming Le Grand Alcazar. The first has stood since 1944, the second since 1943. Both once belonged to great families of Fès nobility, who shaped their decor with the care reserved for homes built to last.

A faithful restoration

  • 1943 – 1944Construction of both houses, in the traditional Fassi style.
  • 2006First restoration of the older house.
  • 2018A further renovation, refining comfort without touching the decor.
  • 2022Complete restoration of the second house.

Every stage called on the craftsmen of Fès: zellige masters, plaster carvers, painted-wood joiners, ironworkers. Nothing was rebuilt without them — zellige reassembled tile by tile, ceilings restored by hand, doors redrawn to the original patterns.

A Fassi family

Le Grand Alcazar is today run by Omar and Othmane, twin brothers born in Fès. Growing up in the medina gave them a love for its old houses, and the conviction that they deserved to be lived in, not just preserved.

Hospitality, the Fassi way

Here, hospitality is as much passed down as learned: a riad is lived at the pace of its patios, its terraces, its quiet. It is this art of welcome, particular to Fès, that the house carries on for every guest.
