Elegance at the heart of the house. Located on the ground floor of Le Grand Alcazar, the Diamant suite charms with its generous proportions and traditional architecture. Its striking six-metre ceiling height showcases a magnificent hand-painted wooden ceiling, a testament to the refinement of Fassi craftsmanship. Its elegant four-poster bed lends the suite an intimate, timeless atmosphere, while its 35 m² offer a comfortable space for a stay as a couple, a family or with friends. Able to accommodate up to four guests, the suite features adjustable bedding and a private bathroom with a shower.
